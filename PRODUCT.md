# Product

## Register

Product — an operational travel-agency platform used throughout the working day.

## Users

Travline serves travel agencies ranging from local specialists to larger regional tour operators, including agencies comparable to Fibula, Funky Tours, Beganović Travel, Tishoury Tours, Dream Balkans Travel, Pohodi, Victorius, 1A Travel, Big Blue Travel, Kontiki Travel, Filip Travel, and Guliver.

Primary users are agency owners, managers, sales agents, operations staff, accountants, guides, and sub-agents. They need one shared operational source of truth without losing the workflows specific to their role or agency type.

## Product Purpose

Travline exists to carry the daily work of a travel agency from inquiry and sale through reservation, payment, departure preparation, execution, reconciliation, reporting, and audit. Packages, departures, travelers, suppliers, accommodation, transport, documents, payments, and operational tasks must remain connected around the same trip context.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, precise, operational, calm, and modern. Travline should feel like dependable professional infrastructure: dense enough for serious work, clear enough to learn quickly, and restrained enough to remain usable all day.

## Anti-references

- Disconnected entity pages that force users to reconstruct a trip manually.
- Generic SaaS dashboards dominated by decorative metrics and empty cards.
- Consumer-travel visual language that weakens operational credibility.
- Feature exposure without workflow logic, permissions, or agency relevance.
- Broad visual redesigns that disrupt familiar operations without measurable benefit.

## Design Principles

1. **Work before reporting.** Prioritize the next operational action, exception, or deadline over passive summaries.
2. **One connected trip context.** A departure is the workspace that connects travelers, money, suppliers, transport, accommodation, documents, and tasks.
3. **Progressive capability disclosure.** Show functions according to agency profile, enabled capabilities, role, and current workflow state.
4. **Preserve operational truth.** Every status, total, warning, and readiness signal must be derived from real data and explainable.
5. **Familiar efficiency.** Keep patterns stable, keyboard-friendly, and optimized for repeated daily use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, complete keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and status communication that never depends on color alone. Interfaces must work structurally on mobile and desktop, support Bosnian and English content, and remain readable with long names, currencies, dates, and operational labels.
